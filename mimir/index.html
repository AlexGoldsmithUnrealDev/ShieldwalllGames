Build deeply. See the connections.

Mimir combines structured entries, visual world tools, narrative planning, AI-assisted understanding, and interactive prototyping in one application. V1 is being built around a simple idea: your world should become more useful as it grows, not harder to manage.

Structured worldbuilding

Build characters, species, cultures, places, factions, artifacts, events, languages, story arcs, quests, dialogue, and more. Your core data remains readable markdown on your machine.

Visualise the world

Follow families, species, cultures, factions, and places through dedicated trees, then use maps, calendars, timelines, backlinks, and relationships to spot connections that walls of text hide.

Mimir AI

Ask questions about your own lore, translate constructed languages, and review canon for contradictions. Mimir retrieves the relevant world context and chooses the appropriate AI processing automatically.

Proving Grounds

V1 adds a dedicated place to prototype and test conversations, quests, tabletop sessions, board-game systems, and other interactive ideas before committing them to the finished work.

Bring your existing world

Import ordinary files and migrate existing worldbuilding projects instead of starting again. Migration is part of Mimir Core because switching tools should not mean abandoning years of work.

Collaborate and host

V1 collaboration supports writers working together, while hosted worlds let a GM remain the power user and invite players as controlled participants or viewers without forcing the whole group into the same workflow.

One Mimir, different ways in

Built for the way you create.

Mimir does not split writers, Game Masters, and developers into different products. The same connected world can move between writing, play, design, prototyping, and production as your project changes.

For writers

Plan narrative arcs, trace families and cultures, track chronology, keep names and canon consistent, prototype dialogue, and collaborate without giving up ownership of your source files.

For Game Masters

Build campaigns, hide spoilers, design quests and conversations, test sessions in Proving Grounds, then host the world for players with controlled access and participation.

For game developers

Keep narrative and world data structured, model variables and state, test quest/dialogue logic, and build a canonical source of truth. Direct MCP and game-engine integrations are on the post-V1 roadmap.

How it works

From scattered notes to a living world.

  1. i

    Start fresh or bring your world with you.

    Create a local lorebible or import existing material. Competitor migration is designed to remove the biggest barrier to switching tools: starting again.

  2. ii

    Structure and connect it.

    Write naturally, link entries, build timelines and maps, and let Mimir turn relationships into something you can navigate visually.

  3. iii

    Ask, test, and refine.

    Ask Mimir what your lore actually says, run consistency reviews, translate languages, and use Proving Grounds to prototype ideas before they become canon.

  4. iv

    Share only what you choose.

    Collaborate with other creators or host a controlled version of your world for players and participants while your primary creative source remains yours.

Mimir AI, without a model menu.

Credits pay for the work Mimir performs, not a particular provider model. Narrow lookups use fewer credits than wide reviews or bulk operations. Mimir chooses the underlying processing route for quality, speed, and cost. Monthly subscription credits reset each billing month; separately purchased top-up credits do not expire and are used after subscription credits.

What does not change when you pay less.

Mimir's commercial model is designed around ownership first. Downgrades may disable premium operations, but they do not erase the world you created.

✦
Unlimited local worlds and entries

Your own files are not a metered cloud resource, so we do not impose artificial local world or entry caps.

✦
Migration and export stay accessible

Moving into Mimir and getting your own data back out are not premium privileges.

✦
Invited participants can use Core

A GM or host can be the power user without requiring every player or viewer to buy the same subscription.

✦
Your local work survives a downgrade

Premium-created local content remains stored and readable. Premium creation or hosted operations may pause until the relevant tier is restored.

Building with a larger professional team?
Loremaster is the V1 high-scale plan. Dedicated Studio/business licensing, advanced team administration, onboarding, and SLA support are future options, not hidden requirements for ordinary creators.

After V1 launches, Shieldwall will spend several months gathering feedback and refining the product before the next major integrations. MCP connections and direct Unreal, Unity, and Godot workflows sit on that future roadmap rather than being promised as launch features.
