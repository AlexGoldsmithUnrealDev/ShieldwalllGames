Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what data Shieldwall Games Ltd collects, how we use it, and what your rights are. Mimir is still in development, but account, AI, collaboration, and hosted features are being introduced during beta. This policy covers those systems as they are made available.

The local-first principle

Mimir is designed so that your primary worldbuilding files live on your own device. Shieldwall does not receive a copy of a local world merely because you create, open, edit, search, map, or browse it in Mimir.

Data leaves your device only when you deliberately use a feature that needs an online service, such as Mimir AI, account services, collaboration, or hosted worlds.

What we collect

Email addresses. If you sign up for the Mimir beta waitlist or the Ashborn launch notification list, we collect your email address and any optional information submitted through that form.

Account and subscription data. When you create or use a Mimir account, Shieldwall processes information such as account identifiers, authentication records, subscription tier, credit balances, purchase entitlements, AI usage records, and security/session metadata needed to run the service.

Hosted project data. If you choose to create or join a hosted Mimir world, collaborative workspace, or hosted Proving Grounds experience, the content and permissions needed for that hosted experience must be stored or synchronised online. This does not turn your unrelated local worlds into hosted data.

Mimir AI request data. When you use an AI-backed feature, Mimir sends the instructions and world context needed to perform that specific operation through Shieldwall's broker to an AI API provider.

Discord integration data. If you connect Discord to Mimir, we may process the Discord user ID, username or display name, profile or avatar information where supplied, friend or relationship information, presence, lobby or call participation, and other limited Discord Social SDK data needed to provide the feature you choose to use.

Session Capture data. If Session Capture is explicitly enabled, Mimir may receive participant-specific voice audio from Discord and process it locally on the host device for transcription. Raw audio is not sent to Shieldwall by default and is discarded after local transcription by default. The resulting transcript may contain participant names, speech, timestamps, and session context.

Basic web analytics and server logs. We may process aggregated website usage, referral information, security logs, IP addresses, and similar technical records needed to operate, protect, and understand the service. We do not sell this information for advertising.

We count how many accounts use Mimir each month. We record only the account and the month, nothing about what you do or write.

How we use your data

We use personal data only for purposes connected to the service you asked us to provide: product communications, account access, subscription and credit administration, fraud/security controls, AI requests, collaboration/hosting, customer support, and service improvement.

We do not sell or rent your personal data, and we do not claim ownership of your Mimir worlds or creative work.

Marketing emails are optional. We only send them if you tick the box when creating your account, and we record when you agreed. You can turn them off at any time in your account settings.

Mimir AI

Customer-facing AI is provided as Mimir AI. Shieldwall may change the underlying provider or model routing over time so long as the data handling remains consistent with this policy and applicable law.

The current development integration uses Anthropic APIs. The final production subprocessor list and retention configuration will be published before public AI billing opens. Shieldwall's production design is to send only the context required for an operation rather than uploading an entire lorebible by default.

Shieldwall does not use your private world content to train its own public generative-AI model. Any third-party AI processor will be selected and configured under the data-processing terms applicable to its API service.

Discord integration and Session Capture

Discord voice and Session Capture are separate features. Ordinary Discord-powered voice is not transcribed merely because a call is active. Session Capture must be explicitly started, and Mimir is designed to make that state clear to participants.

Session Capture transcription is performed locally using Mimir's on-device speech-to-text system. Raw voice audio is discarded by default after it has been processed. The transcript remains local unless you deliberately use an online feature that needs it.

If you choose Analyse with Mimir, the transcript or the relevant selected context is sent through Shieldwall's Mimir AI service so that a summary and suggested new or changed entries can be produced. Those suggestions are presented for review and do not automatically change your world.

Mimir's Discord integration is not available to users under 13. Users must also meet any higher age or other eligibility requirement that applies to their Discord account.

Collaboration and hosted worlds

Hosting is optional. If you use it, Shieldwall must retain the hosted content, permissions, participant data, and synchronisation state necessary to make that shared experience work. The local copy of a world and the hosted copy are conceptually separate: the existence of one does not grant Shieldwall access to every other world on your device.

Retention periods, deletion controls, backup behaviour, and hosted downgrade/archive rules will be documented before hosted billing opens.

Third parties and subprocessors

MailerLite. We use MailerLite to manage mailing lists and product communications. Your email address and mailing preferences may be stored on their systems. You can read MailerLite's privacy policy.

Discord. If you connect Discord to Mimir, Discord provides the account, social, presence, lobby, and voice infrastructure used by those features and may process information under its own privacy terms. You can read Discord's privacy policy.

GitHub Pages. The current public website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process server logs such as IP addresses. You can read GitHub's privacy statement.

Production account, hosting, payment, and AI providers. Mimir uses or may use specialist providers for account data, payments, hosted storage or realtime services, and Mimir AI. We select and configure those services under the applicable data-processing terms and will update this policy where a material provider change affects how personal data is handled.

Payments

Shieldwall does not intend to store full payment-card details itself. Paid subscriptions and purchases are processed through the payment and commerce providers identified at checkout and, where appropriate, in this policy.

Cookies and local storage

The current website does not use advertising tracking cookies. It may use browser storage for functional preferences such as theme or interface state. Third-party embedded services, including mailing forms, may set their own technically necessary storage according to their policies.

Discord data retention and deletion

Discord-derived personal data is retained only for as long as it is needed to provide the connected feature, maintain security, or meet a legal obligation. Disconnecting Discord removes the stored connection credentials from Mimir. Session transcripts are treated as Mimir content and remain under the user's control unless they have deliberately been placed in a hosted feature.

You can contact Shieldwall to request deletion of Discord-derived personal data that Shieldwall holds, subject to any lawful reason that requires a limited record to be retained.

In future we plan to measure how features in the app are used, so we can improve them. That will only happen if you agree: Mimir will ask you, with a clear on and off switch in Settings, and it will never include your worlds or anything you write. We will update this notice and our Privacy Policy before it starts.

Service emails

Our service emails contain no tracking pixels and no tracked links.

Your rights

You can unsubscribe from marketing email at any time using the link in those messages. You can also contact Shieldwall to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, or other rights that apply under data-protection law.

Some records may need to be retained where required for legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, or dispute purposes. Where a request applies, we will explain any lawful reason that prevents immediate deletion of a particular record.

When you delete your account, we keep a scrambled fingerprint of your email address so that free starter credits can only be claimed once. The fingerprint cannot be reversed to recover your email address.

International processing

Some service providers may process data outside the United Kingdom. Where required, Shieldwall will rely on an appropriate lawful transfer mechanism and the provider terms applicable to that processing.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy as Mimir's account, payment, hosted collaboration, AI, Discord, and other online services change. Material changes will be reflected in the "last updated" date and any additional notice required by law.
