Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

This policy explains what personal data Shieldwall Games Ltd ("Shieldwall", "we") collects when you use the Mimir app, your Mimir account and our website, why, who helps us, and your rights.

Who is responsible. Shieldwall Games Ltd, company number 12655497, 17 Chichester Road, Sandgate, Folkestone, Kent, CT20 3BN, United Kingdom, is the data controller. Contact: info@shieldwallgames.co.uk.

1. The local-first principle

Mimir keeps your worlds as files on your own computer. We do not receive a copy of a world because you create, open, edit, search, map or browse it in Mimir. Data leaves your computer only when you deliberately use a feature that needs an online service: your account, Mimir AI, the Marketplace, desktop and phone sync, collaboration, hosted Campaigns or Proving Grounds. Crash reports (section 2.6) are technical only and never contain your worlds.

2. What we collect, and why

2.1 Your account

Your email address, a password (stored only as a secure hash by our account provider), your account settings, your plan, your Mimir AI credit balance and use, packs you own, and security records such as sign-in times and the device session. Why: to run your account and give you what you paid for (contract). Security records: to protect your account (legitimate interests).

We count how many accounts use Mimir each month. We record only the account and the month, nothing about what you do or write. Why: to understand how many people use Mimir (legitimate interests).

2.2 Payments

Our payment provider, Stripe, collects your card details, billing address and payment history. We never see or store your full card number. We receive and keep a record of what you bought, when, the amount, and whether it was refunded. Why: to take payment and keep accounts (contract; legal obligation for tax records).

2.3 Mimir AI

When you ask Mimir AI something, Mimir sends your question and the parts of your world it needs to answer (not your whole world) through our server to our AI provider, Anthropic, and sends the answer back. We keep a record of each request's size and credit cost, not its content. Why: to provide the feature you asked for (contract). We do not use your content to train AI models, and our AI provider does not train on it.

2.4 Sync, collaboration, hosted Campaigns and Proving Grounds

If you choose to host or join a shared world, the content, the member list, roles and changes needed for it are stored online. If you turn on desktop and phone sync, the worlds you sync are kept with your account so your devices can exchange changes. Your other worlds stay local. Why: to provide the shared feature (contract).

2.5 Email

  • Service email (sign-in codes, renewal reminders, billing notices, account deletion notices) is sent to the address on your account. You get these whatever your marketing choice, because they are about your account. Renewal reminders can be turned off in the app. Why: contract.
  • Marketing email is optional. We send it only if you tick the box when creating your account (or join a list on our website), and we record when you agreed. You can turn it off any time in Account → Notifications, or with the link in any marketing email. Why: your consent.

2.6 Crash reports

If Mimir crashes or hits an error, it sends a short technical report to our error-reporting provider, Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.), so we can find and fix the problem. A report contains the type of error, where in Mimir's own code it happened, your Mimir version, and your operating system and its version. It never contains your worlds, entries, file or folder names, or anything you type, and we do not store your IP address with it. Crash reporting is on by default; you can switch it off at any time in Settings → Crash reports, after which nothing more is sent. Reports are kept for 90 days. Why: to keep Mimir working (legitimate interests).

2.7 Updates

When Mimir checks for or downloads an update, it contacts our download server, which (like any web server) sees your IP address and app version. Why: to keep Mimir up to date and secure (legitimate interests).

2.8 Support

If you email us, we keep the conversation and anything you choose to send (for example a problem report) to help you. A problem report Mimir prepares for you lists the screen, the message shown and your Mimir version, and you can read it before you send it. Why: legitimate interests, or contract when it is about something you bought.

2.9 Our website

Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages. GitHub keeps ordinary server logs, such as IP addresses, to run and protect the service. A cookie banner asks what you allow before anything optional runs: our sign-up forms (run by MailerLite) and, later, website analytics that count visits and pages read so we can improve the site and Mimir. See the Cookie Notice below. Why: legitimate interests (running the site); your consent (forms, analytics and mailing lists).

2.10 How you use Mimir (planned, only with your consent)

We plan to measure how features in the app and the website are used, such as which screens are opened and which tools are used, so we can improve them. This will only start with your consent: the website asks through its cookie banner, and the app will ask with a clear on and off switch in Settings. It will never include your worlds, entries or anything you write. We never sell this data or pass it to anyone except the providers that process it for us, who will be named on our Subprocessors page before it starts. Why: your consent.

2.11 Discord (when available)

Discord features are not yet available. When they are, connecting Discord will be optional and this policy will say exactly what Mimir receives.

6. Your rights

You can ask to see the data we hold about you, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or receive it in a portable form. You can export your account data yourself from Account → Data & account, and delete your account from the same place. To use any other right, email info@shieldwallgames.co.uk. We reply within one month.

Where we rely on your consent (marketing email), you can withdraw it at any time.

If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, please tell us first. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk), or to your local data protection authority if you live in the EU.

7. Children

Mimir accounts are for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child has created an account, email us and we will delete it.

8. Security

Connections to our services are encrypted. Passwords are stored as secure hashes by our account provider. Access to personal data inside Shieldwall is limited to the people who need it to run Mimir. Your worlds are on your own computer, so their security also depends on your device.

9. Changes

We will update this policy when Mimir changes. We will tell you about important changes by email or in the app before they apply, and update the date at the top.
