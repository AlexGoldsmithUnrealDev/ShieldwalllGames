In developmentCore

Automation proposes. The creator decides.

Spreadsheet conversion itself is deterministic. An optional Mimir AI step can suggest matches between source columns and your existing fields, but it does not create entries or silently choose your canon.

For prose documents, AI-assisted extraction proposes named and described entities. Every proposal remains reviewable and becomes a follow-up draft only after confirmation.

  • Recognised: file type, columns, rows and readable prose
  • Suggested: optional column matches or prose entry proposals
  • User-selected: entry type, name, mappings and which proposals survive
  • Not silently converted: unsupported or unmapped values

04
Review before you commit

The uncertain parts should stay visible.

Review concrete conversion limits and choices before they become draft entries. Mimir should not hide uncertainty behind a reassuring progress bar.

Inspect what happened and why.

What was found

A spreadsheet row has no usable name.

Why it matters: Mimir needs a name to create an identifiable entry.

Verified behaviour: The row is skipped rather than becoming an unnamed entry.

What was found

An imported name already exists.

Why it matters: importing should not overwrite existing material.

Verified behaviour: a unique identifier is generated for the new draft.

What was found

A text column targets an image or relationship-list field.

Why it matters: plain spreadsheet text cannot safely recreate those structures.

Verified behaviour: those field types are skipped by the current converter rather than guessed.

What was found

AI-assisted prose extraction proposed an entry.

Why it matters: extraction is lossy and a suggestion is not canon.

Your control: rename, re-type or remove the proposal before creating draft entries.

This inspector explains verified conditions and safeguards. It is not a claim that the product currently presents this exact warning interface.

05
See the result before you trust it

Nothing is created until you choose to create it.

The current flows separate reading and review from the explicit action that creates draft entries.

Verified workflow boundary

Follow the information from source to draft.

  1. 01SourceKeep the original attachment
  2. 02ReadParse rows or extract text
  3. 03MapChoose fields or inspect proposals
  4. 04ReviewEdit, omit or confirm
  5. 05Create draftsExplicit final action

The original source is present. No Mimir entries have been created.

What this does not claim

This is not a promise of a universal dry-run transaction, one-click rollback or perfect recreation of another tool. After import, Mimir's normal local files and history/recovery systems apply to the entries you created.
