The studio

Alive, imaginative,
and world-led.

This should not read like a generic company website. Shieldwall is an independent creative studio: part worldbuilding workshop, part game studio, part invitation to imagine what comes next.

Shieldwall Games exists to build connected creative experiences. We are not trying to look like a giant publisher with a huge catalogue. We are building one world at a time, with a long view of where those worlds can go.

Our site should carry that same energy. It should feel active, atmospheric and filled with possibility — driven by artwork, strong mood, and the sense that each section opens another door into the same creative universe.

Our first product

Vitus opens the gate.

Vitus is the first step in the wider vision: a worldbuilding platform that lets creators organise, connect and explore their ideas without surrendering control of their work.

Replace with Vitus landscape artwork

We wanted to release the tool before the rest of the universe fully arrives. That way, players, GMs and writers can begin building their own stories immediately instead of waiting passively for ours.

  • Launch phase: Vitus launches first and establishes the creative foundation.
  • Beta period: approximately six months of beta, shaped by feedback and refinement.
  • Version 1.0: targeted for 2027, marking the first major milestone for the wider Shieldwall ecosystem.
Roadmap

Three clear horizons.

The roadmap should be simple, readable and visually grounded in artwork. The aim is not to overwhelm visitors — just to show where Shieldwall is going next.

Now →
2027

Vitus launch & 1.0

Vitus launches first, then spends around six months in beta as we refine the experience, gather feedback and push toward a full 1.0 launch in 2027.

First major milestone
2027

Ashborn CCG

While Vitus develops, we continue work on Ashborn CCG with the aim of launching toward the end of 2027. It expands the same broader creative world into a collectible card game format.

Second major milestone
After

Ashborn — Saga's Tavern

After Ashborn CCG comes the next major standalone game: Ashborn — Saga's Tavern, an RPG simulator with major Vitus integration, where a GM can create scenarios and encounters with ultimate control over the session — like a story-driven RPG where your own world and roleplayed cutscenes matter.
