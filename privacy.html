Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This policy explains what personal data Shieldwall Games Ltd (“Shieldwall”, “we”) collects through this website and the Mimir application, why, and what your rights are. Shieldwall Games Ltd is the data controller for the processing described here. Mimir is in its pre-release period; this policy describes the systems that exist now, and anything not yet live is labelled as such.

The local-first principle

Mimir is designed so that your worldbuilding files live on your own device. Shieldwall does not receive a copy of a local world merely because you create, open, edit, search, map, chart, or browse it in Mimir — those are local operations on your own files, and we cannot see them or inspect your local worlds.

Information leaves your device only when you deliberately use a capability that needs an online service: signing in to a Mimir account, buying something, asking Mimir AI a question, or writing to support.

What we collect

Website and mailing lists. If you join the Mimir beta waitlist or the Ashborn launch list, we collect your email address, any optional information the form asks for, and your mailing preference. Marketing is always separate from service email: creating a Mimir account never adds you to a mailing list, and unsubscribing from marketing never stops the service email your account needs.

Mimir account. When you create a Mimir account we process your email address, a unique account identifier, your email verification state, the security and session records needed to operate sign-in, and your account standing — including, if you request account deletion, the request and its scheduled date.

Purchases and billing. For paid plans and purchases we process your subscription state (plan and billing period), references that link your account to our payment provider, your Mimir Credit balances and history, your one-time purchases (including, for Content Packs, the record of your agreement to immediate digital supply), and your Content Pack licences. Shieldwall never receives or stores full payment-card details — cards are handled entirely by Stripe.

Mimir AI. When you use an AI feature, Mimir sends your instruction and the selected parts of your world needed for that specific operation through Shieldwall's service to our AI provider, together with the metering records needed to account for usage. Requests are retrieval-bounded: Mimir sends selected context for the operation, not your whole world by default.

Support. If you contact us we process your message, your contact details, and the information needed to resolve your issue.

Service and security records. The infrastructure that runs our online services processes technical logs — such as IP addresses and request records — needed to operate and protect those services. The Mimir desktop app does not send analytics or telemetry beyond the online operations described in this policy, and this website carries no advertising tracking. We do not sell personal data.

Hosted collaboration (not yet live). Optional collaboration and hosted-world features are planned but not part of the current product. If and when they launch, this policy will be updated first to describe exactly what hosted content is stored; nothing is collected for them today.

Why we process it, and our lawful bases

Providing your account and its services — sign-in, subscriptions, credits, purchases, licences, the AI features you invoke, and support — is performance of our contract with you.

Payment administration and financial record-keeping rests on that contract and on the legal obligations that apply to accounting and tax records.

Security, fraud prevention, and service integrity — including security logs and abuse controls — rest on our legitimate interest in running a safe service.

Marketing email is sent only with your consent, and you can withdraw that consent at any time with the unsubscribe link in any message.

Mimir AI and its provider

Customer-facing AI is provided as Mimir AI. AI requests are processed by Anthropic, our AI provider, under the data-processing terms applicable to its API service; Shieldwall may change provider over time so long as the data handling remains consistent with this policy and the change is reflected here.

Shieldwall does not use your private world content to train a Shieldwall public generative-AI model. What we send to the provider is what the operation needs: your instruction and the selected world context, never a routine upload of your lorebible.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers to run Mimir and this website. In the app itself these appear simply as Mimir features; they are named here because transparency about processors is what a privacy policy is for.

Supabase — account authentication and the account database (sign-in, account standing, entitlements, credits, licences).

Stripe — payments, subscriptions, and billing. Stripe processes your card and holds its own records of your payments under its own legal obligations; Shieldwall holds references to those records, never your card. Stripe's privacy policy.

Cloudflare — the server infrastructure our online services run on, and delivery of purchased Content Packs.

Anthropic — processing of Mimir AI requests, as described above.

MailerLite — mailing lists only (the beta waitlist and launch lists). Never account or service email. MailerLite's privacy policy.

A transactional email delivery service — used solely to deliver service email about your account (for example, confirmation that an account deletion was requested, cancelled, or completed). The specific provider will be named here when that delivery service is switched on for the beta.

GitHub Pages — hosting for this public website, which may process server logs such as IP addresses. GitHub's privacy statement.

International processing

Some of these providers process data outside the United Kingdom. Where they do, transfers take place under safeguards recognised by UK data-protection law — such as UK adequacy regulations or the approved standard contractual clauses and UK addendum included in each provider's data-processing terms.

How long we keep things

Active accounts. Account data is kept for as long as your account exists and needs it.

Account deletion. You can delete your Mimir account from inside the app. Deletion is scheduled 30 days out — a product default, shown to you as an exact date — and you can cancel it any time before then. When it completes, your account identity and the account data it owns are erased.

What survives deletion. Some financial and audit records — purchase history, credit accounting, and the record that a deletion happened — are kept with your identity removed from them, only for as long as needed for accounting and tax obligations, fraud prevention, security, resolving disputes, and enforcing legal rights. Records held by Stripe about your payments remain with Stripe under its own retention obligations. Your local worlds were never account data and are not touched by deletion.

Service email. Delivery records for account service email keep the recipient address only for a bounded operational window (currently 30 days after delivery or final failure), after which it is stripped.

Mailing lists. Marketing data is kept until you unsubscribe or ask us to remove it.

Backups. Our database provider maintains short-term backups for disaster recovery. Data erased from the live system leaves those backups as they rotate, and is not restored into active use.

Your rights

Under UK data-protection law you can ask for access to your personal data, correction, erasure, restriction of processing, to object to processing, and for a portable copy of data you provided. These rights depend on context and are not absolute — where a legal obligation requires us to keep a record, we will explain that when we answer.

Mimir includes a self-service Export account data feature (on the Account page) that saves the data your account holds as a readable file. It is a convenience, not a substitute: you can always make a full subject-access request by email instead.

To exercise any right, email alexander.goldsmith@shieldwallgames.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) — though we would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

Cookies and local storage

This website does not use advertising tracking cookies. It may use browser storage for functional preferences such as theme or interface state. Third-party embedded services, including mailing forms, may set their own technically necessary storage according to their policies.

Changes to this policy

When the product changes in a way that affects personal data — a new processor, a new online feature — this policy is updated first. Material changes are reflected in the “last updated” date and any additional notice required by law.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: contact Shieldwall Games Ltd at alexander.goldsmith@shieldwallgames.co.uk, or see the support page for what to include.
