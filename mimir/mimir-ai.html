02
Grounded in your canon

The answer starts with your world.

Mimir retrieves the lore relevant to the task instead of sending a large world's entire lorebible with every question.

Retrieval can consider the current entry, linked entries, relevant lore found elsewhere in the world and the question being asked. Focused mode keeps the entry you started from in the foreground while still allowing connected material to be found.

The model is only one part of the feature. Mimir's structure, relationships, retrieval, context selection and citations shape the answer before it reaches you.

  • Current or focused entry
  • Connected and retrieved lore
  • The current question or operation
  • Evidence that can be traced back to entries
03
Know what is known

Knowing the answer means knowing when there isn't one.

Mimir is designed to distinguish established canon from supported inference and information the world simply does not contain.

Conceptual response states

Question: Who killed King Aldric?

Canon
“King Aldric died at Greywatch in 614 AR.”

This statement is explicitly established in the available world entries.

Inference
“House Vael may have benefited from Aldric's death, but the current canon does not confirm their involvement.”

The conclusion is plausible from available material, but it is not a canonical fact.

Unknown
“The current world does not specify who delivered the killing blow.”

There is not enough supported material to answer. The honest response is to leave the gap visible.

The honesty invariant

No invention when the answer is missing.

Factual claims should trace back to the creator's lore. Reasoning should be identified as reasoning. Unsupported gaps should remain gaps.

This is designed behaviour, not a claim that any generative system is infallible. Evidence and creator review still matter.

◇The creator remains the authority.

05
Language & context

Choose the material. Preserve the rules.

Focused context and authored language systems give creators explicit control over advanced AI work.

Available in alphaAI Context Bundles · Worldsmith+

Take a deliberate slice of the world with you.

In the current alpha, AI Context Bundles turn a saved, spoiler-aware filter into one readable Markdown file for an external AI tool. They are the explicit export path, separate from Ask Mimir's automatic retrieval.

A focused bundle keeps unrelated lore out of the task. Choose an era, culture, campaign, region or faction, then retain control over the wrapper instructions and where the file goes.

In testingConstructed-language Translator · Core

Your language should remember its own rules.

The user defines the vocabulary, grammar, registers, cultural bias, calibration phrases and writing system. Mimir applies that authored material to the translation and lets newly coined words be reviewed before they become canonical.

This is not generic phrase substitution. Register, idiom and accepted vocabulary can shape each result.

Illustrative language example
EnglishWelcome home, old friend.
Formal registerVa elen thara, vener sael.Respectful distance and full honorific.
Familiar registerEla thar, saelen.Warm, shortened phrasing between equals.
