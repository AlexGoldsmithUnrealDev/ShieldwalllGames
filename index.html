The studio

Alive, imaginative,
and world-led.

Shieldwall is a small independent studio in Kent: part worldbuilding workshop, part game studio, and an open invitation to imagine what comes next.

Shieldwall Games exists to build connected creative experiences. We’re not a giant publisher with a huge catalogue. We’re building one world at a time, with a long view of where those worlds can go.

We started with the tool we needed ourselves. Keeping a world consistent across years of lore, characters, maps and timelines is hard, and nothing we tried kept up with our own games. So we built Vitus, and we’re putting it in your hands before anything else.

Our first product

Vitus opens the gate.

Vitus is the first step in the wider vision: a local-first worldbuilding platform that lets writers, Game Masters and game developers organise, connect and explore their ideas without giving up control of their work.

We wanted the tool out before the rest of our world arrives, so players, GMs and writers can start building their own stories now instead of waiting for ours.

  • Open beta: Vitus is out now for Windows, with the free Core plan: unlimited local worlds and entries, kept as ordinary Markdown files on your own computer.
  • Beta period: around six months, shaped by your feedback and bug reports, with regular updates.
  • Version 1.0: targeted for 2027, the first major milestone for the wider Shieldwall world.

Roadmap

Three clear horizons.

Three releases, in order. The dates are targets rather than promises, and we’ll share more as each one firms up.

  1. Now →
    2027

    Vitus open beta & 1.0

    Vitus is out first, in an open beta of around six months while we refine it with your feedback, on the way to Vitus 1.0, targeted for 2027.

    First major milestone

  2. Late
    2027

    Ashborn CCG

    While Vitus grows, we’re developing Ashborn CCG, a historically grounded Viking Age collectible card game built with Vitus. We’re aiming to launch it towards the end of 2027. More about Ashborn

    Second major milestone

  3. After

    Ashborn — Saga’s Tavern

    Next comes Ashborn — Saga’s Tavern, a standalone, GM-led RPG simulator with major Vitus integration. The Game Master shapes the scenarios and encounters for their friends and keeps control of the session. Its mechanics and release date are still to be decided.
