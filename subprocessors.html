Subprocessors

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Shieldwall Games Ltd uses the providers below to run Vitus. Each one processes personal data only to provide its service to us, under a data processing agreement. We will update this page before adding a provider that handles personal data.

Your worlds are files on your own computer and are not sent to any of these providers unless you use a feature that needs it (shown in the table).
