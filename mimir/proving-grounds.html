02
Conversations

Hear the conversation before the audience does.

Explore branching dialogue in the context of the characters and world it belongs to, while every result remains a draft experiment.

Planned for public betaApprentice+

Dialogue testing is intended to help Game Masters prepare difficult NPC exchanges, writers explore alternative responses and narrative designers inspect branches before anyone encounters them in a finished work.

Mimir's authored Dialogue graphs support speakers, choices and condition-gated branches. The public Proving Grounds workflow will bring that structure into a clearer testing experience.

Describe the logic. Then challenge it.

Mimir's Quest graphs can represent start and end points, choices, conditions, consequences, dialogue, quest markers and referenced subgraphs. Testing is intended to expose missing connections and unexpected routes before a live story or campaign depends on them.

04
World state

Choices matter when the world remembers them.

Variables and conditions describe the facts that can change during a test, so later branches can respond to what happened earlier.

In developmentWorldsmith+

World state is different from permanent canon. During a test it can hold a temporary value such as an open gate, an alliance, a living commander or a fallen road, then let the next part of the experiment react.

Mimir's authored variable system supports typed global and scoped values. The full public workflow remains under development, and this demonstration deliberately uses simple on/off states.

06
The creator decides

An experiment can inform the world without taking control of it.

Run the test, inspect what happened and learn from it. The creator decides whether to revise the world, discard the result or deliberately carry an idea forward.

Creator authority

The tool can reveal a possibility. It cannot decide what your world should be.

Run test→Inspect result→Learn→Choose

Proving Grounds complements real playtesting and creative judgement. It does not perfectly predict players, guarantee balance or automatically modify canon.
