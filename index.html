The studio

Tools first.
Worlds always.

We are a small, independent studio in Kent. We make worldbuilding tools and games that share one creative vision, and we take the long view with all of them.

We started by building the tool we needed. Keeping a world consistent across years of lore, characters, maps and timelines is hard, and nothing we tried kept up with our own games. So we built Vitus, and we are putting it in your hands before anything else.

Every Shieldwall release stands on its own, with its own rules and its own way to play. They all grow from the same imagination: Vitus is where worlds are written, and Ashborn is where you will first be able to play in ours.

  • Your work stays yours
  • Depth that earns its place
  • Self-funded and independent
  • Made in Kent, UK

Our first release

Vitus opens the gate.

Vitus is a local-first worldbuilding platform for writers, Game Masters and game developers. It now has a home of its own at brothervitus.co.uk.

  • Build your world as connected entries, kept as ordinary Markdown files on your own computer.
  • See it through family and culture trees, maps, calendars and timelines.
  • Plan story arcs, quests and dialogue, and step through them before your players do.
  • Share it by hosting campaigns, writing with others in the Writers’ Room and asking Vitus AI about your own lore.

The free Core plan is yours to keep, with unlimited local worlds and entries. The public beta opens soon and runs for around six months, then Vitus 1.0 is targeted for 2027.

Roadmap

The road ahead.

Three releases, in order. The dates are targets rather than promises, and we will share more as each one firms up.

  1. Beta soon
    1.0 in 2027

    Vitus

    Vitus comes first. The public beta opens soon and runs for around six months while we listen, refine and fix. Vitus 1.0 is targeted for 2027.

    Our first release

  2. Late 2027

    Ashborn CCG

    A historically grounded Viking Age collectible card game, in development alongside Vitus and built with it. We are aiming to launch it in late 2027. More about Ashborn

    Our first game

  3. After
    Ashborn CCG

    Ashborn — Saga’s Tavern

    A standalone RPG simulator built around the Game Master, with major Vitus integration. The GM sets the scenarios and encounters and keeps full control of the session, so your world and your table’s roleplay drive the story.

    No date set yet

Future worlds

Stories still to tell.

Beyond these three releases, the worlds keep growing. We would rather show you the shape of what is coming than pretend any of it is finished.

  • Narrative plans

    Two trilogies

    At least two narrative trilogies are planned for our future games. They are a long way off, and we will say more when there is something real to show.

  • Shared vision

    One world, many games

    Each game has its own rules and its own way to play. Together they grow from one creative vision, so the more you play, the more of the world you will know.

  • Your stories first

    No waiting room

    You should not have to wait for our stories to tell your own. From the day the beta opens, Vitus is there for your worlds, your campaigns and your ideas.

Creativity should never be held back.

It is the belief behind everything we make. We release our tools before our games so that writers, Game Masters and developers can build, test and tell their own stories while ours are still on the way.
