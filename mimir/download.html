Download Mimir

Local-first worldbuilding for Game Masters, writers and developers. Your world lives on your machine as plain files you control.

Download for Windows

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. About 150 MB. Windows only for now.

Your browser may warn you

Mimir is new software and not yet widely downloaded, so your browser may show a warning. This is normal.

  1. Edge: click the three dots ... next to the download, then Keep, then Show more, then Keep anyway.
  2. Chrome: click Keep.

Install

  1. Open Mimir-Setup.exe.
  2. A blue "Windows protected your PC" box may appear. Click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Mimir installs by itself and opens when it is ready.

First launch

  1. Read the licence and click I accept.
  2. Sign up with your email address.
  3. Tell Alex the email you used so he can add your tester plan.

Test payments

This is a test server. No real money is ever taken.

Most testers get a free tester plan from Alex. If he asks you to try a purchase, use this test card:

  1. Card number: 4242 4242 4242 4242
  2. Expiry: any future date, e.g. 12/34
  3. CVC: any three digits, e.g. 123
  4. Postcode: anything
Never type a real card number. This is a test environment.

Back up your worlds

Your worlds are plain files in the folder you chose when you created them. Copy that folder somewhere safe now and then. That is all you need to do.

Updates

Mimir checks for updates every hour. When one is ready, "Update ready · Restart" appears in the top-right corner. Click it to update, or the update installs automatically the next time you close Mimir.

Report a bug

  1. In Mimir, open Community, then Feedback.
  2. Say what you did, what you expected, and what happened.
  3. A screenshot helps. Press Windows + Shift + S to take one.
  4. If Alex asks for the log file, it is at: %APPDATA%\Mimir\logs\mimir.log
