Support

Mimir support during the beta

Support runs by email while Mimir is in its pre-release period. Write to alexander.goldsmith@shieldwallgames.co.uk for anything on this page — it goes to the people building Mimir, and we aim to reply promptly. We are a small team, so we do not publish a guaranteed response time.

General Mimir help

Start with the in-app guides: Help in Mimir's sidebar covers every feature, from your first character to the timeline and the AI. If a guide does not answer it, email us with what you were trying to do and what happened instead.

One thing worth knowing before any support question: your worlds are files on your own machine. No account, billing, or service problem can take away your local work — everything offline keeps working regardless.

Billing and payment problems

Wrong charge, failed payment, plan not activating after checkout: email with the address on your Mimir account and roughly when the payment happened. Payments are processed by Stripe, so a receipt or the last four digits of the card can help us find it — never send your full card number or your password.

Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel yourself, in the app: Marketplace → Subscription → Cancel subscription stops the renewal, and your paid access continues to the end of the period you already paid for. Your local worlds are unaffected. If anything about cancelling looks wrong, email us and we will sort it from our side.

Refunds and consumer rights

Your statutory rights always apply — we do not operate a “no refunds” policy. If something you bought is faulty or not as described, tell us and we will put it right. First-time subscribers can ask for a no-questions refund within 14 days of their first subscription. The details of how cancellation rights work for subscriptions, Content Packs, and credit purchases are in the Terms of Use; when in doubt, just email — asking costs nothing.

Account deletion

You can delete your Mimir account from inside the app: Account → Delete this account. Deletion is scheduled 30 days out, you see the exact date, and you can cancel it any time before then with one click. Your local worlds are never part of account deletion. If you cannot access the app but need your account deleted, email from the address on the account.

Your data and privacy

The Privacy Policy explains what we hold and your rights. In the app, Account → Export account data saves everything your account holds as a readable file. For access, correction, erasure, or any other data-protection request, email us — we answer these ourselves.

Content Pack purchases and downloads

Bought a pack that will not download or install? Email with the pack's name and what the app showed. Downloads can be re-fetched on any machine you sign in on; installs never overwrite your world without a backup, so nothing is lost while we sort it out.

Security concerns

If you think you have found a security problem in Mimir or our services, email us directly with the details and give us a chance to fix it before sharing it publicly. If you think someone else has accessed your account, change your password from the sign-in screen's password reset and email us straight away.

What to include in a support message

The email address on your Mimir account; what you were doing; what you expected; what happened instead, with any exact error text; and for app problems, the build line from the error screen if one is shown. Never include your password, recovery codes, or full card details — we will never ask for them.

Priority support

The LOREMASTER tier includes a priority support queue. That means prioritised handling — LOREMASTER messages go to the front of the queue — not a guaranteed response within a set number of hours.
