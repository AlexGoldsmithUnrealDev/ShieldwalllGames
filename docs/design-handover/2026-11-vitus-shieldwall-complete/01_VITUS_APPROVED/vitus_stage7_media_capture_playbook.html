RULE 01 / CONSISTENCY
Use one demonstration world.

The location, characters, dates, maps, manuscript and AI answers should all belong to one canon. Real connections are the selling point.

RULE 02 / READABILITY
Write for small screens.

Record at high resolution, but crop to one clearly readable action. Never try to squeeze a full 4K desktop interface into a phone-sized video.

RULE 03 / MOTION
Let visitors control the pace.

Silent ambient loops may autoplay only if subtle and muted. Features, carousels and explanatory video do not advance automatically.

04 / Technical and creative standards

One consistent language.
Every asset, every screen.

DESKTOP APP CAPTURES

Record clean at 2560×1440 or 1920×1080, 30fps, with consistent app theme and 110–125% readable UI scaling where practical. Keep 8–15s micro-demos concise.

SMALL-SCREEN COMPOSITIONS

Make dedicated portrait/4:5 crops rather than shrinking whole desktop recordings. Keep the key app control visible within the first view. Hero brand illustration can disappear.

STILLS & DELIVERY

Keep archival masters as lossless PNG. Deliver compressed WebP/AVIF images at responsive sizes. Provide a deliberately chosen poster image for every video.

VIDEO DELIVERY

Export edited MP4/H.264 with a WebM variant where beneficial. Keep muted autoplay loops short and optional, with lazy-loading below the fold. Avoid baking tiny captions into the pixels.

BRAND ART DIRECTION

Maintain Iron & Ink: dark iron, oxblood, parchment and gold. Original graphic-novel-style fantasy artwork should be clean and crisp; avoid generic stock and inconsistent painterly styles.

CONTENT TRUTH

Use screenshots of an actual, tested Vitus build. No invented features, counterfeit testimonials or inaccurate pricing. Recheck Claude’s feature list and commercial rules when we reach the copy stage.

ACCESSIBILITY

Videos have text alternatives/captions, visible playback controls, keyboard support and a still poster for reduced-motion. Functional information cannot depend solely on animation.

FILE HYGIENE

Use a stable ID in every filename, e.g. F03-writing-desk-desktop-master.mp4. Version source and derived crops separately; remove private account names and machine paths.

05 / What gets captured first

Produce media in the order
the website needs it.

PHASE 1 / NOW
Assets independent of unfinished features

Curate an attractive example world, Brother Vitus character crop, studio/project artwork, and the media composition templates. Decide names and art direction before recording.

PHASE 2 / AS WORKFLOWS PASS QA
Capture repeatable user actions

Record hero app views, Build, Own, Writing Desk, Map and AI workflows. Use fresh builds and repeat the captures whenever UI changes materially.

PHASE 3 / PRE-LAUNCH
Assemble and verify

Produce the short trailer, poster images and responsive crops; then check all feature claims, captions, accessibility and performance on the actual website.

Proposed Stage 7 sign-off

Approve asset placement, scope and capture rules now. Final clips and screenshots can follow the actual app, with feature copy deliberately left until last.
